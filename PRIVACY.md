# Privacy Policy

## Cal Schedule ICS Extension

### Data Collection

**Cal Schedule ICS does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.**

This extension:
- Processes your schedule data locally on your computer only
- Does not send any information to external servers
- Does not track usage or analytics
- Does not use cookies or tracking pixels
- Does not access any data beyond the currently active Berkeley schedule page
- Does not require an account or login

### What the Extension Does

1. When you click the "Export" button, the extension reads only the Berkeley schedule table from the page you're currently viewing
2. It extracts course information (name, time, days, location, instructor) from that table
3. It generates an ICS calendar file on your computer
4. The file is downloaded to your device—no data leaves your computer

### Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or external connections.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this file. Your continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of any changes.
